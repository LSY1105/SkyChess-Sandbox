THE LATEST AVIATION NEWS FROM AROUND THE WORLD
Volcano Eruption Causes Flight Chaos
Press release1st April 2028
Mount Zog, an active volcano in the vicinity of Ellington, erupted last 
night without warning. Residents have been safely evacuated and 
there is no immediate threat to life. However, the business park has 
been severely damaged, and Ellington’s famous beaches have been 
contaminated by ash and lava.
It is certain that normal business and leisure activities in Ellington 
will be disrupted for some time to come. The demand for air 
transport is likely to be affected, and all airlines serving Ellington are 
expected to suffer over the next three months.
