THE LATEST AVIATION NEWS FROM AROUND THE WORLD
Major Hailstorm Hits Hub Airport
Aircraft Damaged
Press release1st July 2028
A major hailstorm swept through the Hub Airport yesterday 
evening leaving a trail of destruction. Giant hailstones damaged 
buildings, motor vehicles, as well as aircraft standing in the open 
at the airport. Fortunately, no-one has been reported injured.
Airlines that have invested in hangars were spared the worst of 
the damage. However, Capital Airways’ APT100 was parked in the 
open and took the full force of the storm, which inflicted damage 
to the wings, fuselage and engine cowlings. The aircraft requires 
extensive repair and are expected to be grounded for a period of 
six weeks. Daily utilisation must therefore be limited to no more 
than 4 hours per day for the next three months.
