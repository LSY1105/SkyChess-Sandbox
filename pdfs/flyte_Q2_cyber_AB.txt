THE LATEST AVIATION NEWS FROM AROUND THE WORLD
Hackers Target Air Ballistic
Press release
Media sources are buzzing with the news that Air Ballistic has been 
subjected to a cyber-attack. It appears that attempts to encrypt 
customers’ personal information, including passwords and credit card 
data, have been successful. The airline was also robbed of over 30GB 
of source code, compromising their revenue management system. 
There is no doubt that passengers are unhappy with the situation.
The consulting group Red Queen Consulting claims to have alerted 
Air Ballistic to the potential risks of a cyber attack. An unnamed 
source suggested to Flyte Magazine last night that the airline is being 
held to ransom.
Air Ballistic will hopefully make a full statement, including a list of 
measures they will adopt to ensure that such a breach will not 
happen again. 
1st April 2028
